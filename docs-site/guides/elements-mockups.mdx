Both tools are in the image toolbar under **⋯**.

## Edit Elements

**Edit Elements** takes a finished image (a poster, an ad, a screenshot of a design) and rebuilds it as an editable artboard:

1. Every line of text is read with on-device OCR ([RapidOCR](https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR)) and becomes a real text layer at the same position, size, color and weight.
2. The main product and logo are cut out as separate image layers with SAM3.
3. The text and objects are erased from the picture with LaMa, leaving a clean background layer.

The new artboard appears next to the original. Change the wording, font or color, move the product, then click **Re-render** in the artboard toolbar to blend the edited layers back into one natural-looking image.

<Note>
Edit Elements needs `rapidocr` (installed by `requirements.txt`), plus the SAM3 and LaMa models used by the other image tools. See [Models](/guides/models#image-tools).
</Note>

## Mockups

**Mockup** turns a product photo (a t-shirt, mug, bag, box, phone) into a surface you can print a design on. ComfyArt measures the photo's depth and cuts out the product once; after that, placing the design is instant and runs in your browser.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/mockup-panel.webp" alt="Mockup panel placing a badge logo on a t-shirt" />
</Frame>

In the Mockup panel:

- **Design**: pick any image from the canvas. Logos with a transparent background work best; run **Remove Background** first if needed.
- **Left–right**, **Up–down**, **Size** and **Angle** place the design.
- **Follow surface** bends the design with the folds and curves of the product. The product's own shading is laid over the design, and anything outside the product is masked away.
- **Bake as image** flattens the mockup into a normal image.
- **AI blend** re-renders the result with the image model for more realistic fabric, print texture and lighting.

The mockup stays editable: select it again to swap the design or move it.
